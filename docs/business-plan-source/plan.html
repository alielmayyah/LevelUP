The problem

  • Gyms lose many members in the first months. Every lost member is lost monthly revenue.
  • Body composition scans produce numbers members don't understand or revisit.
  • Small gyms have no affordable, branded way to engage members between visits.

The solution

  • FitScore: one private 0–100 score from every scan.
  • 50 medals with XP reward lifts and body-scan milestones.
  • Missions, leaderboards and a pixel avatar give members a reason to come back.

How it works

1 · Scan

Member does a body composition assessment at the gym.

2 · Score

App turns it into a FitScore and a mission the member picks.

3 · Play

Lifts and scan milestones unlock medals and XP.

4 · Stay

Leaderboards and challenges keep members coming back.

03 · Market & Competition

Who we sell to, and why we win.

Arabic-first fitness software is thin. Cairo gyms are priced out of global platforms that don't gamify body composition.

The buyer

Owners of independent gyms and small chains (1–10 branches) that already run body composition scans.

  • Cares about renewals and referrals
  • Has a scanner but no engagement tool
  • Wants a branded app without building one

The user

Gym members, mostly 18–40, smartphone-first, Arabic- and English-speaking.

  • Wants to see real progress
  • Motivated by medals and ranking
  • Free to use, no password to remember

07 · Risks & Next Steps

Know the risks. Move in 90 days.

Slow gym adoption

High

Free pilots, low entry price, and retention data that proves the return.

Scan data entry friction

Medium

Simple front-desk flow, bulk import, manual entry fallback.

One-founder bandwidth

Medium

Tight MVP scope; white-label config instead of custom builds.

Member data privacy

Low

FitScore private by default, minimal personal data, clear consent.
