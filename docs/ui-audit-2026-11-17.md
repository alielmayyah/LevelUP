# UI Audit: 17 Nov 2026

Four read-only audits of the app's code, each through a different set of skills from the owner's "UI Skills" folder:

| Lens | Skills used |
| --- | --- |
| Polish and motion | make-interfaces-feel-better, Emil Kowalski, transitions.dev |
| Hierarchy, spacing, type | Refactoring UI, Impeccable, Swiss design |
| Usability and accessibility | Web Design Guidelines (Vercel), userinterface.wiki |
| Taste and anti-slop | Hallmark, Taste, Frontend Design |

Every audit also checked the owner's product rule: **simple and minimal by default for members who don't know much, with an opt-in way for detail lovers to see more.**

## What everyone agreed on

1. **Progress is overloaded.** It shows about 9 numbers, 9 changes and 9 info buttons before the member taps anything. Only body composition and the charts sit behind "View more details".
2. **The same fact is shown several times.**
   - Mission card: ring, bar, done/total and "kg to go".
   - Medals overview: count, percent, then a stats row repeating both.
   - FitScore card: tier chip plus tier meter.
3. **Empty states break the rules.**
   - Home's monthly wins show three "—" tiles when there is no scan this month.
   - An empty mission card can't be tapped.
   - The empty Progress page has no next step.
4. **FitScore, the headline number, is never explained.** "Needs Improvement" reads as a judgment.
5. **Purple gradients and glows on every action** (CTA, tab pill, bars) are the most recognizable "AI UI" tell, so they stop meaning anything.

## Changes in this pass

**Simple by default, details on request**

- **Progress:**
  - Shows the scan date, the three key numbers (weight, muscle, body fat) and their change.
  - Everything else sits behind one "See full scan" control: the six extra measurements, the compare picker, body composition and trends.
- **Medals:**
  - The overview is the count and one bar.
  - Lifting jargon and the working-set and pending lines move into each medal's sheet.

**Home**

- FitScore gets an info sheet: what it is, the five tiers, how to raise it and the privacy note. "Needs Improvement" becomes "Getting started".
- **Mission card:** shows the ring, the title and "kg to go". With no goal, the whole card opens New goal.
- **Monthly wins:** with no scan this month, one plain line replaces the three dashes.

**Kit and every screen**

- **Color:** the CTA, tab pill and progress bars are solid brand purple. There are no colored glows.
- **Labels:** no uppercase micro-labels, and no decorative icons in front of headings.
- **Motion:**
  - Dialogs and toasts animate in and out.
  - Presses ease instead of snapping.
  - Icon buttons have a disabled look.
- **Taps and loading:**
  - Small Settings pills meet the 44 px tap target.
  - Medals loads with skeletons.
- **Avatar frames:** the dark avatar frames become a soft, light stage.

**Words**

- Plain names: "Belly fat (around organs)" instead of "visceral fat", and "الـ BMI" in Arabic.
- Clearer FitScore setting hint.
- Fit ID length in the login help.
- A sign-out warning about the Fit ID.
- "Pick scan" instead of "Change".
- One Arabic "back".

**Goals**

- Only one primary button on the empty screen.
- A toast confirms a new goal.

## Later (not in this pass)

- **Home hero around level, XP and today's habits.** This belongs to the solo milestone, which rebuilds Home anyway.
- **A condensed display face** for big numbers, with an Arabic pairing.
- **Removing or editing a goal.** This needs an API endpoint.
- **Type scale tokens.** About 35 one-off text sizes should collapse to about 8 steps (0.75 / 0.875 / 1 / 1.125 / 1.25 / 1.5 / 2 / 3 rem) app-wide.
- **New medal artwork.** The medal images are still pixel art.
- **A real brand moment on the splash** instead of the starfield.
