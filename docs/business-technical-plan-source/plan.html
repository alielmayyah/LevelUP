PROBLEM

Members can't read their sheet

The gym pays, the member doesn't

Problem and market

Egypt prints InBody results on paper

InBody's cloud sync (LookinBody Web) is only offered in the United States. Nobody fills the gap in Arabic, so results stay on paper or in phone photos.

Members

Can't read the sheet, can't compare scans, lose old papers, and give up when the scale doesn't move even while they improve.

Gyms

Members who don't see progress cancel. The scan earns no follow-up sale. Staff explain sheets one by one, or not at all.

Trainers and nutritionists

Explain the same numbers to every client and track progress in notebooks or WhatsApp photos.

03

How the business works

B2B2C: the gym pays, the member gets the experience

We sell to the business (B2B). The business gives the app to its customers (B2C). The member's great experience is what makes the gym earn more, and that is why the gym keeps paying.

Who pays

Gyms and clinics, monthly per branch. Nutritionists and small clinics pay per scan. Members pay only if their gym isn't on the app yet (Member Plus).

Who uses it

Members: timeline, reports, compare.
Staff: upload and follow-up.
Trainers: their clients and leads.
Owner: stats and money earned.

Why it sticks

The member's whole history lives in LevelUP, next to the gym's logo. Leaving means members lose their timeline and the gym loses its lead list. That's a strong reason to stay.

04

What the gym gains

The member gets the experience.
The gym gets money it can count.

The gym is the one paying, so its gain has to be concrete. The benefits, ranked by how much an owner will care:

STRONG · MONEY THE OWNER CAN COUNT

1. More paid re-scans

Many gyms and clinics charge for each InBody scan. Today a member scans once and forgets. With the app, they get a "time for your next scan" reminder every 4–6 weeks and can see their progress, so they come back. At about EGP 150 a scan, 7–10 extra re-scans a month already cover the EGP 990 plan. This is the best pitch: direct, fast and easy to prove in a 60-day pilot.

2. Personal-training and nutrition sales

The "muscle loss", "imbalance" and "no scan in 60 days" flags give trainers a list of people to approach, each with a reason: "Your scan shows you lost 0.8 kg of muscle. Let me help you fix that." One PT package sold usually pays for months of the subscription. Anything that produces leads for trainers sells.

3. Progress-tracking packages

The gym can sell a "3-month transformation" bundle (3 scans, the app report, a trainer review) for more than single scans. The app is what makes that bundle feel like a real product.

REAL · HARD TO PROVE QUICKLY

Retention. Members who see progress cancel less. Use it as the story, not the proof, because 60 days is too short to show it.

Free marketing. Share cards carry the gym's logo to Instagram and WhatsApp.

Looking modern. "We have our own app" helps gyms compete.

WEAK · KEEP OUT OF THE PITCH

No paper, less staff time. Paper costs almost nothing and many gyms don't explain sheets anyway. It's a member benefit, not a gym saving.

The gym pitch · part 2

The money line, objections and the close

4 · The money line (use their own numbers)

"كل عضو هيجيله تذكير يعيد القياس كل 4 لـ 6 أسابيع، والمدربين بتوعك هياخدوا ليستة بالناس اللي محتاجة برايفت، ومع كل اسم السبب. لو 8 أعضاء بس رجعوا يقيسوا في الشهر، الاشتراك دفع تمنه."

"Every member gets a reminder to re-scan every 4–6 weeks, and your trainers get a list of people who need PT, each with a reason. If just 8 members come back for a scan each month, the plan has paid for itself."

Objections and answers

They sayYou answer
"My staff are too busy."One upload takes under a minute, or they upload everything once at the end of the day. Nothing to install.
"Members won't download an app."Their result is already waiting when they log in with their phone number. There's a QR code at the desk and a web version for people who won't install.
"We already have gym software."We don't replace it. We're the part it doesn't do: explaining the scan and turning it into sales. We're working on integrations with Fit Boost, Rakiz and SigmaGym.
"It's expensive."Run the math with their scan price. Then offer the 60-day free pilot: "If it doesn't bring you more than it costs, don't pay."
"What about member privacy?"Members give consent, the gym only sees its own scans unless the member allows more, and every action is logged. You can delete any data.
"InBody has its own app."Its cloud sync isn't available in Egypt, it isn't in Arabic, and it doesn't carry your logo or give your trainers leads.

5 · The close: a free pilot with a price agreed in advance

"جرّبه 60 يوم ببلاش. هنتفق دلوقتي على هدف واضح وعلى السعر بعد التجربة. لو ما جابلكش أكتر من تمنه، ما تدفعش."

"Try it free for 60 days. We agree now on a clear goal and the price after the pilot. If it doesn't bring you more than it costs, you don't pay."

The pilot agreement (one page)

  • Goal: e.g. +20 re-scans or 10 PT leads in 60 days
  • Price after day 60: written in, with a pilot discount
  • Gym does: upload every scan the same day, put up the QR sign
  • We do: onboarding visit, import history, weekly check-in
  • Day 60: review the numbers together, convert, and ask for 2 referrals

Design first

Prototype #1 is the experience, not the code

The first thing we build is a clickable, animated design prototype. Opening the app should feel like stepping into another world: calm, beautiful, and instantly clear. We only start coding once real members understand their results from the prototype in seconds.

Design principles

  • One answer per screen. "Did I improve?" is answered in 3 seconds, before any number.
  • Words before numbers. The headline is a sentence in Egyptian Arabic; numbers support it.
  • Calm dark canvas. One accent colour for progress, amber for attention, never alarming red on someone's body.
  • Motion that means something. Numbers count up, rings fill, a new scan arrives with a reveal moment.
  • Arabic-first, not translated. Designed RTL from the start, with a real Arabic typeface.
  • Fast. Opens in under a second, works offline with the last data, no spinners, only skeletons.

The "new result" moment

The most important 5 seconds in the app. A push arrives: "نتيجتك الجديدة وصلت". Tapping it opens a full-screen reveal: the background glows, the headline fades in, the main change counts up (+0.8 kg muscle), a light haptic tap, then the 3 key changes slide up.

This is the moment members screenshot and share. It's also why they come back for the next scan.

Quality bar

Match the polish of the best health and finance apps (Apple Fitness, Oura, Revolut), applied to Arabic and to InBody data. Learn from them, don't copy them.

How we test the prototype (weeks 1–2)

STEP 1

Design

Figma, Arabic-first. Real data from the friend's two scans.

STEP 2

Animate

Figma Smart Animate + ProtoPie for the reveal; Lottie files for celebrations.

STEP 3

Test

5 members and 2 front-desk staff, on a real phone. Watch them; don't explain.

STEP 4

Gate

5 of 5 explain their progress in their own words in under 10 seconds. Staff upload a scan with no help.

The same prototype is the sales demo. While coding starts, use it to sign the first 3 pilot letters.

"This isn't my scan"

Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (151/2020)

Body-composition results are health data, which the law treats as sensitive. Get a lawyer's advice before the first paying gym. This is a summary, not legal advice.

  • Explicit written consent from the member
  • A licence or permit from the PDPC
  • Guardian consent for children under 15
  • Hosting outside Egypt counts as a cross-border transfer
  • Serious penalties: plan as if the law is enforced now

Medical claims and trademark

  • Fitness information, not diagnosis. No disease names; flags say "consider talking to a doctor". Disclaimer on every report.
  • LevelUP keeps "InBody" out of its name and logo. Say "works with InBody, Tanita and other scan sheets".

How the design responds

  • Consent when the gym registers the member and again at first login, with timestamp and version
  • Separate consent for each gym that can see the member's data
  • Minimum data: phone, first name, sex, birth year, height. No national ID
  • Result images encrypted, behind expiring links. No third-party AI on scan data
  • Export and delete buttons for members
  • Data processing agreement with every gym (gym = controller, we = processor)
  • Privacy notice in Arabic and English
  • Every query filtered by organization, with automated isolation tests
  • Roles with least access; every upload, edit and move logged
  • Ask the lawyer: licence type and fees, hosting location, scans saved before sign-up
Register first: Commercial Register and Tax Card before taking money. Paymob and invoicing both need them.
