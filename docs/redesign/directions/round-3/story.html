LevelUP

Story

Home reads like a short story about your body, told top to bottom in cards: what changed, what your body looks like today, what you earned, when to come back and how, and who to talk to. Each card has an icon header and exactly one thing to do. One shape carries the brand across the app: the story arc, a gradient arc on a true time scale with a dot for every scan and a dashed end for the next one.

Mood
Narrative, warm, sure-footed
Type
Readex Pro (300–700), one family for Arabic and Latin. Its Latin is Lexend, a geometric sans that matches the logo's wordmark, and its Arabic is geometric too.
Palette
The shared Round 3 tokens only. Gym brands swap the brand layer; meaning colors never move.
Signature
Story arc: oldest scan → today → next scan, right to left in Arabic

Screens

Phone frames are 390 × 844 and scroll inside (try it). Arabic RTL unless marked. Every number has the example's real value.

Gym re-coloring

Each column is the same markup inside a wrapper: <div data-gym="ironbox">. The wrapper swaps only the brand layer: --brand, --brand-strong, --brand-soft, --brand-tint, --on-brand and the gradient stops. The green chip, the muscle and fat dots and the grey "about the same" chip are the same in all three.

Components

Every card in the feed uses one anatomy: an icon chip, a title and subtitle, the body, then one action row at the bottom. Icons are Lucide shapes at a 1.9 stroke in 40 px tinted containers. Data icons take their data color, and brand icons take the brand tint.
