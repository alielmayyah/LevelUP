Body First

Karim's own body is the interface. Home is a well-drawn, front-view figure whose arms, legs and trunk fill to his percentage of ideal lean mass; the verdict sits above it and the changes that matter are callouts pinned to the body. Tap any part of him, or any callout, and a sheet explains that number. The same figure returns in the report, body balance, compare and the new-result reveal, so the app has one recognisable picture instead of a dashboard.

AnatomicalCalmTactileDark violet, gym-swappable

Screens

Arabic RTL unless noted, 390 × 844. The figure is drawn once (front view, never mirrored: Karim's right arm is on the viewer's left, and every side is named in words) and reused on five screens. Segments fill from the feet up to his % of ideal lean mass on a 0–110% scale; the dashed line inside each segment is 100%.

Gym re-coloring

One wrapper attribute, <div class="app" data-gym="ironbox">, swaps only --brand, --brand-strong, --brand-soft, --brand-tint, --on-brand and --brand-gradient. The gym badge, button, avatar ring, chip and active tab follow; the body, the good-news green and the stable grey are identical in all three. White fails on both gym brands (2.87:1 orange, 2.57:1 teal), so those gyms get dark --on-brand #0C0A14 (6.85:1 and 7.66:1).
