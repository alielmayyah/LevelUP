Widgets

Modular · scannable · yours

Home is a grid of widgets in three sizes, like a phone's home screen. One large widget answers "did I improve?" with the verdict sentence and a gradient journey ring; a wide one shows weight as fat plus fat-free mass; small ones give each metric its own tile with an icon, the value, a sparkline of real scans and a status chip. Every widget is a door: tap it and its detail opens from the same shape. Members can rearrange the grid, so the app grows with what they care about.

Font: Readex Pro (Arabic + Latin, one geometric family that echoes the logo's rounded wordmark), 300–700. Shared round-3 palette only.

Gym re-coloring

Only the brand layer changes, through custom properties on a wrapper (data-gym="ironbox"). Rings, buttons, the active tab and the gym badge follow; the green, grey, muscle and fat chips stay identical. White fails on both gym brands, so those gyms set a dark --on-brand.

What the shared palette couldn't do

Measured WCAG 2.x ratios; how this direction works around each one.

  • White on --brand #8B5CF6 is 4.23:1, under 4.5 for normal text. Filled buttons and the on switch use --brand-strong #6B3BF5 (white 5.88:1). Brand-colored text is always --brand-soft (7.26 on surface-1, 5.89 on brand-tint), since --brand as text drops to 4.05 on surface-2 and 3.64 on surface-3.
  • No text color works across --brand-gradient. White on #E19CFB is 2.03 and on #B98CFF 2.54; dark #0C0A14 on #6B3BF5 is 3.34. So the gradient never carries text: it is the ring stroke, the icon puck inside the primary button (dark icon on the light end, 9.68), and the "UP" of the wordmark.
  • --brand-strong fails as a graphic on raised surfaces: 3.14 on surface-1, 2.92 on surface-2, 2.62 on surface-3. It is a fill only, never a stroke or icon.
  • --text-faint #6F6987 is never text: 3.56 on surface-1, 3.31 on surface-2, 2.97 on surface-3. It is used only for the small open-arrows and the off-switch border on surface-1/2 (≥3:1 as graphics).
  • --border is 1.32:1: decoration only. Anything that must be seen as a boundary (switch, edit-mode outline, normal band) uses faint, brand or stable instead.
  • --stable as text is 4.47:1 on brand-tint (passes on surface-1 to 3 at 5.50–4.60). Grey chips write their words in --text-muted (5.78 on the chip tint) and keep stable for the band and dots.
  • --lean next to --fat is 1.19:1. The weight split bar keeps a 3 px surface gap so each segment stands on surface-1 (lean 10.89, fat 9.12) and has a labelled legend.
  • White fails on both gym brands: 2.87 on Iron Box #FF6A1A and 2.57 on Pulse #12B5A6. Both gyms set a dark --on-brand (#1A0A00 at 6.74; #021A17 at 7.04), and their derived --brand-strong (#E0550A, #0E9A8E) was picked so dark text still passes on the button fill (5.03, 5.19).
