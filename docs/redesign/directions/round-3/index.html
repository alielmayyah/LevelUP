Same dark palette from the logo, same member and numbers. The brand colors re-color per gym (see the orange Iron Box and teal Pulse Gym strip in each); muscle, fat, water and the "improved / attention / about the same" colors never change. Pick the concept, or mix parts of them.

Story

Home is a feed of cards, each with an icon header and one action: the verdict, your body today, a new record, the next scan with a countdown, your coach. Signature: a gradient "story arc" with a dot per scan.
