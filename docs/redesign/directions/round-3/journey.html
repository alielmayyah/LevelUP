Level Journey

Progress is a road, and every InBody scan is a stamped stop on it. Levels are earned only when the body really changes, beyond the machine's measurement noise (muscle about 0.9 kg, fat about 1.5 kg on an InBody 770). Opening the app, streaks of logins or tapping buttons never earn anything.

Home leads with the current level and the verdict sentence, then the path so far and the next stop. A new scan that clears the noise becomes a level-up moment; a flat scan gets a calm "on the way" stamp. The honest data (ranges, noise, the original sheet) is always one tap from any badge or number.

Green for real gains, grey for "about the same", and the muscle/fat colors are identical in all three.

Rules and checks

How a level is earned

  • Level 1 is the first scan. Each further level is one real change since the first scan: muscle up by the noise floor (≈0.9 kg), or fat down by its floor (≈1.5 kg). Karim: muscle +1.0, fat −2.3, so level 3.
  • Smaller changes count only when 3+ same-machine scans move one way and the total clears the floor.
  • No level, badge or reveal across machines, for weight loss alone, or for anything done in the app.
  • The next level is stated as a body target ("muscle +0.9 kg more"), never as a progress bar that would invite reading noise.
  • Levels never go down on screen; a later loss shows in the verdict and the trend, in neutral or amber words.

What didn't work in the shared palette

  • White on --brand #8B5CF6 is 4.23:1, under 4.5 for button text. Default CTA moved to --brand-strong (5.88).
  • The violet gradient can't carry any text: white runs 2.03 to 5.88 across it, dark #0C0A14 runs 9.68 to 3.34. It's used only on the emblem rim, the road, the tab indicator and the wordmark.
  • --text-faint #6F6987 fails as text (3.78 on bg, 2.97 on surface-3); used only for dashed graphics and the locked-badge outline.
  • --brand-strong as a graphic drops to 2.92 on surface-2 and 2.62 on surface-3; kept as a fill under white text only.
  • --stable on --brand-tint is 4.47, so grey "about the same" text never sits on a tinted card.
  • Iron Box orange collides with --fat (#FF9A5C soft vs #F2A65A), and Pulse teal sits near --water and --good. Brand color therefore never touches a metric: data always carries its own icon and word.
