Clinic Calm

Trustworthy, clear, unhurried.

A good clinic never raises its voice. The verdict comes first in plain words, then each number sits quietly on its normal band, the way a good doctor walks you through a chart. Paper white and stone grey hold the page. One teal green means “do this” or “better”, and amber is saved for the rare thing that needs a look. Hairlines and space replace cards and shadows.

Palette

Every ratio is WCAG 2 contrast, computed from the hex pair. Text pairs clear 4.5:1; data colors, the normal band and the body figure clear 3:1 on the background they sit on. Muscle, fat and water step in lightness as well as hue (light: OKLCH L 0.43, 0.54, 0.61; dark: 0.67, 0.80, 0.89), so they separate without relying on red against green.

Type

Noto Sans Arabic for Arabic and Public Sans for Latin, both variable (100–900) from Google Fonts. Noto Sans Arabic ships 256 Arabic code points with init, medi and fina shaping; Public Sans carries every digit in both languages, with tabular figures. Public Sans is listed first, so digits and Latin names always come from it and Arabic falls through to Noto. Numbers are set light and large; words carry the weight.
