A scan read like a session log after the last set: graphite floor, white type, and one volt-lime signal that lights up only when something really improved.

Numbers are set big in Kufam, a square Kufi face that reads like scoreboard lettering in both scripts. Everything else stays grey on purpose, so the lime keeps its meaning.

Palette

One accent, one attention color, three data colors, one neutral for “about the same”. Ratios are WCAG 2, computed from the hex pairs and rounded down. Text needs 4.5, numbers at 24 px or more and graphics need 3.

Data colors split on lightness as well as hue: in dark, muscle is the darkest of the three, then fat, then water; in light, fat-free mass is near-ink so it never depends on hue against fat. Muscle (blue) and fat (sand) sit on the blue–yellow axis, which survives red–green color blindness. Amber appears only as a filled chip with dark text, never as a line. No red anywhere on the body.

Type

Kufam for headlines and every number: geometric Kufi, square terminals, tabular figures by default. Vazirmatn for reading text: full Arabic and Latin, tabular figures. Both from Google Fonts, both with real Arabic glyphs. No letter-spacing on Arabic.
