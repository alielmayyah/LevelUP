Soft Mint

  • Approachable
  • Optimistic
  • Supportive

A scan should feel like a kind coach checking in, not a lab result. A pale mint canvas, white pebble cards and pill controls keep every screen calm; deep teal carries each action and each real gain, and apricot only asks for attention. Baloo Bhaijaan 2’s round Arabic makes the verdict sound spoken, not printed. Pastels never carry text: every word meets measured contrast.

Signature: the bead on a soft track. Each value is a ringed dot resting on a rounded rail, the normal band a mint pill inside it. One shape language, from the range bar to the tab bar.

Palette

WCAG 2 ratios, computed from the hex values. Text pairs need 4.5:1; data marks, range-bar edges and the body figure need 3:1. Muscle, water and fat sit on three lightness steps, so they separate in greyscale and without red vs green: muscle is always the highest-contrast mark, fat the quietest.

Type

Baloo Bhaijaan 2 is round in both scripts and ships tabular figures, so it sets the verdicts and the numbers. Rubik’s softened corners carry body text and UI at small sizes. Both are on Google Fonts with full Arabic subsets. Arabic gets about 10% more leading and is never tracked.
