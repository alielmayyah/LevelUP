# LevelUP app audit (19 November 2026)

**Why this exists.** The owner reviewed the redesigned app on 18 November and rejected it: "I don't like the UI, the splash screen, the transitions or the layout. This is a really bad UI/UX experience, and there are a lot of mistakes everywhere." On four screenshots:
- **Home hero:** "too much text here".
- **"Your body today":** "this doesn't make sense at all".
- **Account milestones (Level 2 … Level 9):** "this is really bad too".
- **Account "More":** "this should be called settings".

**How it was done.**
- **Screenshots:** 128 captures of every screen in Arabic and English, dark and light, 320 px and 390 px, plus frame strips of the splash, a tab switch and a page push.
- **Five parallel audits**, each led by the vendored UI skills and the global `ui-ux-pro-max` data:
  - **Visual:** frontend-design, interface-design, no-ai-design-slop, learn-taste, better-colors, better-ui.
  - **UX, structure, copy and data meaning:** better-ui, state-machine, break-ui, ionic-mobile-experience.
  - **Layout, type, color and accessibility:** better-layout, better-typography, better-colors, better-accessibility.
  - **Motion and app start:** animate, choreograph, animation-performance, review-animations, gsap-*.
  - **Code and consistency:** ui-standards, break-ui, state-machine.
- **Contrast:** every ratio here was computed.
- **Bundle:** measured with a scratch build.

The implementation plan that fixes all of this is [`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-19-redesign-v2.md`](../superpowers/plans/2026-11-19-redesign-v2.md).

## 1. Verdict: why it reads as "really bad"

1. **It's a template, not a product.**
   - Every tab is a stack of equal 20 px cards, each opening with the same 40 px icon tile, title and subtitle (`FeedCard.tsx`).
   - Home has 6 cards and 7 tiles; the scan report has 11 tiles. Nothing leads.
   - Skills: interface-design "one focal point", no-ai-design-slop "card apocalypse".
2. **Violet everywhere means violet signals nothing.** Violet is 78–100% of all tinted pixels on Home, Scans, Compare and Account. It comes from:
   - the violet-tinted neutrals,
   - a radial violet glow behind every page (`layout.css` → `--lu-page-bg`),
   - violet tiles, initials, milestone rows and goal bars.
3. **Too much text, no hierarchy.**
   - Home carries about 155 words.
   - The hero says "since your first scan" twice and runs 6 lines at 320 px.
   - The verdict (22 px) is smaller than the greeting (24 px).
   - The same change appears four times: as a sentence, as the road, as two rows and as chips.
4. **The data contradicts itself.** For Mohamed (101, 8% body fat):
   - Home says "Fat −4.7 kg · Improved", "You reached level 9" and "Next level: fat −1.3 kg".
   - The very next card says "your body fat is 8%, under the healthy 10%".
   - The report calls +0.9 kg muscle "about the same"; the old Progress page, one tap away, calls the same change "Stronger and leaner".
   - The reveal can celebrate a worse result, in green.
5. **Two apps stitched together.** These screens are still the old glass, aurora and gradient-pill look:
   - Splash, Intro, Login.
   - Settings, Goals, New goal, Notifications.
   - Profile, Medals, Character, "Full details".

   The new tabs link into them.
6. **Motion makes every action feel slow.** Start-up passes through 4–6 different screens:
   - native splash → light flash → web splash with a fake progress bar and a chime → zoom-out → blank → skeleton → content.

   After that:
   - **Tab visits:** every visit, including Back, blanks the page and fades sections back in (`useReveal`).
   - **Tab bar:** it bounces (420 ms overshoot plus a 460 ms icon hop).
   - **Presses:** stacked elastic GSAP presses on top of CSS presses.
   - **Page push:** on Android, both pages are visible on top of each other.

## 2. Findings by area

Severity: **P0** = the owner sees it and it's wrong · **P1** = broken or off-system · **P2** = polish.

### 2.1 Data meaning and rules (all P0/P1)

| # | Where | Today | Rule that replaces it |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | `api/mock/journey.ts` verdict and levels | Fat loss is always "better" and earns levels, even at 8% body fat | Range-aware. **Muscle:** a real gain is better; a real loss is "watch". **Fat:** above the middle of the normal band (men 15%, women 23%), a real loss is better and a gain is "watch". Between the middle and the low edge, changes are neutral. Below the low edge (men 10%, women 18%), a loss is "watch" and a gain is better. "Steady" means only "inside the noise". |
| D2 | `journey.ts` `nextLevel` | Asks a member below range to lose 1.3 kg more fat | Offer only healthy-direction targets. For 101 that is muscle only. |
| D3 | `journey.ts` levels | Unbounded; a cut that loses real muscle still earns fat levels; two levels from one scan show as two rows; the first scan is "Level 1" | A level = one real healthy-direction change since the first scan, same machine. No level at a scan where the other metric had a real loss. The first scan is "Start", not a level. Levels never go down. Two levels at one scan show once ("Level 3, +2"). Re-scored this way, 101 becomes Level 5 and 104 becomes Level 2. |
| D4 | `home/revealRules.ts`, `Reveal.tsx` | The reveal plays for worse changes and paints the "biggest change" green | Play only for a real better change or a new level. Color comes from the verdict. A worse result gets a calm amber card with the coach button. |
| D5 | `body/advice.ts`, `progress/insight.ts` | Old 0.2/0.3 kg "real change" thresholds drive "Your next step" and the old Progress page | One rules engine with one set of thresholds (0.9 kg muscle, 1.5 kg fat, 1.9 kg weight, 1.9 points fat %), owned by the API. Delete the client copies once the mock owns them. |
| D6 | Weight chip | Always "About the same", even for +3.8 kg | Weight is never good or bad. Beyond 1.9 kg say "Up 3.8 kg" in neutral; within it say "Steady". |
| D7 | Rounding | "+0.9" displayed but judged "about the same" (0.8999) | Compare the rounded, displayed value with the threshold. |
| D8 | Fat % change | "−1.3%" | Percentage points: "−1.3 pts" / "−1.3 نقطة", or "20.4% → 19.2%". |
| D9 | Range words | "A bit high" for muscle above range and for high water | Graded words: Normal / A bit high / High. Muscle above range = "Above average". Water gets no word unless it is low. |
| D10 | Changes with no baseline | "Muscle +4.9 kg" sits under "Your body today · Nov 15" | Every change names its baseline ("since June", "vs October"). |
| D11 | Road | Its height is a sine wave (`Road.tsx:15`), a fake chart that always climbs | Remove from Home. Scans uses real data lanes on a straight date axis. |
| D12 | Trend flags | Brand-colored flag on any exit from the band, losses included | Flag healthy-direction exits only; amber dot otherwise. |
| D13 | Body balance | The ±2% "balanced" zone (≈0.2 kg) is inside the limb noise (≈0.3 kg arm, ≈0.6 kg leg); 2-decimal values | Balanced unless the gap beats the limb noise on two scans in a row. Always show a word. 1 decimal. |
| D14 | Goals | English titles in the Arabic UI; two active goals; weight goals; "lose 2 kg fat" offered at 8% body fat | One goal, muscle or fat only, healthy direction only, in steps of the real-change floor. The title is built in the app from `I18nText`. |
| D15 | Copy | "Raises the risk of heart problems" is still in the plan copy | "Fitness information, not a diagnosis". No disease names. |
| D16 | Different machine | Judged first scan vs last, so a member who changed gyms long ago is flagged forever; cross-machine changes show "About the same" next to +2.5 kg | Judge latest vs previous. Add a `directionOnly` verdict with its own chip. |
| D17 | Seen-scan logic (`Dashboard.tsx`) | One key for all members, compared with `!==`; any old SCAN_RESULT notification opens today's reveal; a member who installs with 0 scans never gets their first reveal | Store per member and fire only for a newer scan (better: API `seenAt`). Notifications carry a target and open their own report. |
| D18 | Places on Account | "Your gym shows up after your first scan" shown to a member with 6 scans | Separate states: loading, no scans, scans without a place. |

**Bundle.** Initial JS is **209 KB gzip, over the 200 KB budget**, plus 21 KB of CSS. Where it goes:
- Both locale JSONs load up front, and 73% of the Arabic one is old namespaces.
- Alexandria downloads on first paint for the tab labels and titles.
- GSAP (27 KB) loads with Home only because NotificationsSheet calls `useReveal`.
- Unused assets ship: IBM Plex woff2 (44 KB) and `logo.png` (205 KB).

Lazy-loading the old namespaces, the GSAP fix and font subsetting should bring it to roughly 175–185 KB gzip (estimate).

## 3. What the owner already said (proposed taste rules for `learn-taste`)

| Owner said | Rule |
|---|---|
| "too much text here" | One headline per screen (≤ 8 words in English, ≤ 10 in Arabic); ≤ 25 words per module; no subtitle that restates its title; each number appears once per screen; explanations behind a "؟" sheet |
| "this doesn't make sense at all" | Every change names its baseline; one body concept per module; no number without its meaning word |
| "this is really bad too" (the levels list) | No lists of identical rows; achievements are stamps with what earned them |
| "this should be called settings" | A destination named exactly **الإعدادات / Settings** |
| "I don't like the splash / transitions" | No web splash; tabs never replay; no bounce on daily UI; motion only on the new-result moment |
| "keep these, we'll use them later" (18 Nov) | Old screens and styles stay in the code under `src/legacy/`, out of navigation, not deleted |
